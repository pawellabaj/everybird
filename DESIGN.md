# Design System: Everybird

Recorded from the shipped code (as of 2026-11-05): `src/styles/tokens.css`, `globals.css`, the component CSS, `collage-motion.ts`, `src/lib/share/image.ts`. Where the code and an intention differ, this file says what the code does and flags the gap under "Known gaps".

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Sticker Sheet on Warm Paper"**

Everybird is an Operate surface (a tool that does one job) wearing Yusuf's personal-site clothes. The birds are the only colour and the only illustration; everything else is warm paper, near-black ink, one deep red and hairlines. The interface recedes so the collage leads.

**The match-/lifelist principle.** The collage page mirrors Yusuf's `/lifelist` (read-only reference: `framer-site/code-components/LifeList.tsx`, `PlacesMap.tsx`): the same dock, the same filter chips (no icons on filter chips), the same card (layout C), the same list rows, the same search (copied from his Photography page), the same motion constants. When the two differ, `/lifelist` wins unless Yusuf says otherwise. Do not "improve" a shared pattern on one side only.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Light by default, never the OS setting; dark only by the visitor's toggle.
- Flat: hairline borders, no shadows at rest. Shadows exist only on floating things (card, tooltip, results list, toast, sheet).
- One accent (a deep red) for selection, focus and the primary action. Rarity is the point.
- Superellipse corners, 44px touch targets, 16px gutters, everything works at 320px.
- Mono uppercase labels for numbers and meta; Cabinet Grotesk for display; Satoshi for everything else.
- The pages are Collage (`/`), Settings (`/settings`), About (`/about`), plus the shared view `/s/[token]`.

## Colors

Seven tokens, two themes, nothing else (the one exception: the Red List Exception below). `tests/tokens.test.ts` keeps `tokens.css` and `SHARE_COLORS` (share image) equal.

| Token | Light | Dark | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | #1e1e24 | #ffffff | Text, the count, icons |
| Paper | #f6f4ef | #1e1e24 | Page and floating-surface background; text on Accent |
| Accent | #92140c | #f06a5f | Primary button, selected chip border, selected tab text, focus ring, links in notes, errors |
| Muted | #5f5f69 | #a9a9b2 | Secondary text, counts on chips, mono labels, placeholder |
| Line | #857f74 | #76767f | Input borders, checkbox borders, switch track outline, hover borders |
| Hairline | #dedad2 | #3d3d45 | Every divider and resting border |
| Surface | #ebe8e1 | #28282f | Chips, quiet buttons, fields, selected tab, disabled fill |

- **Theme mechanism.** `:root` is light; `:root[data-theme=dark]` overrides. There is no `prefers-color-scheme` rule. `src/lib/theme.ts` stores the choice per device (`localStorage` `everybird:theme`); `THEME_SCRIPT` in `<head>` sets `data-theme` before first paint. Share images and the poster follow the share dialog's "Dark image" switch, which starts at the current theme.
- **The One Voice Rule.** Accent marks "this one": the selected chip, the current tab, the primary action, the focus ring. Never decoration, never two accents.
- **The Red List Exception.** The seven tokens are the interface's only colours, except the IUCN Red List chart's eight category colours (LC #60C659, NT #CCE226, VU #F9E814, EN #FC7F3F, CR #D81E05, EW #542344, EX #000000, DD #D1D1C6). They are data, not voice, and appear only in the status pill: the bird card and the hover tooltip (its own line under the other name), the Status panel's rows, and (2026-11-15, Yusuf) a small pill on threatened birds (VU, EN, CR) in the collage and the list view (`StatusMark.tsx`). They never appear on the share images, link previews, search results or the chips, or as decoration. They are the same in both themes. The text is `#1e1e24`, or `#ffffff` on CR, EW and EX (AA on every fill, `tests/iucn.test.ts`). A 1px Line ring keeps the edge at 3:1 on Paper in both themes. Accent is still the only interface accent.
- **The Quiet Disabled Rule.** Disabled is Surface fill, Hairline border, Muted text. Never a paler Accent (it reads as a new colour).
- **The Contrast Rule.** Muted on Paper and Accent on Paper meet AA in both themes. Line, the border that must be seen (fields, the switch's off-track ring in the dock and Settings rows, checkboxes, the dropzone), meets 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) against both grounds it sits on: light 3.61:1 on Paper and 3.25:1 on Surface; dark 3.69:1 on Paper and 3.25:1 on Surface (`tests/tokens.test.ts` checks all four). Dark Line was #6e6e78 (2.9:1 on Surface) until 2026-11-06, when Yusuf asked for 3:1 on grey; lifting the one token, rather than adding a border token, keeps every Line use (hover borders, the on-list tick box, empty-month ticks) one step above Hairline in both themes. Hairline stays decorative (dividers, resting borders) and is not held to 3:1.
- **Browser surfaces.** Selected text is tinted with Accent (`::selection`: Accent mixed 22% into transparent, text keeps its colour) and text carets are Accent (`caret-color` on inputs and textareas), in both themes (`globals.css`).
- Scrims are `rgb(0 0 0 / .28)`, except the first-visit welcome's, `.12`, so the sample stays in view behind it. Shadows are black at 8%/4% (Elevation below).

## Typography

**Display:** Cabinet Grotesk 700. **Body:** Satoshi (500 base, 600/700 for emphasis). **Mono:** Geist Mono via `next/font/google` (`--font-geist-mono`). Satoshi and Cabinet Grotesk load from Fontshare's CSS and are never committed (also drawn on the share canvas via the same families).

### Hierarchy (as shipped)
- **Page title** `.eb-pagehead-title` (Cabinet 700, `clamp(32px,5vw,48px)`, line-height 1.05, -0.02em): one page head (`PageHead`) for Collage, Settings and About, on a `.eb-page-titled` page: the same top offset (24px, 40px from 720px) and gap to the content (24px, 32px from 720px) on all three. `.eb-title` (`clamp(44px,9vw,72px)`, .95) is not used by the page heads.
- **Collage hero / head** (Cabinet 700, `clamp(28px,4vw,42px)` landing hero; `clamp(24px,4vw,40px)` the birder's collage title, verbatim, or "My collage").
- **Section heading** `.eb-h2` (Cabinet 700, `clamp(24px,4vw,28px)`, 1.15).
- **The count** `.eb-num` (Cabinet 700, `--f` x 1cqw, five digits at 85% (`--fs`), line-height .85, tabular numerals) over the 12px `.eb-mono` "SPECIES", 6px below: the species number at the centre of the collage, in a hole the packer keeps empty around exactly this text (`src/lib/layout/count-box.ts`: the shown count's width from measured glyph advances, the label at the layout's narrowest stage, grown by 0.17 F with round corners), so no sticker ever sits on "1,089" and a three-digit count's hole is where the old ellipse was. The hole is the same in every language: the number is drawn in the UI language ("1,089", es/pt/nl "1.089", whose "." is no wider than "," in any face) but sized from its en-US text, and the label (`count.label`, upper-cased: "SPECIES", "ESPECIES", "ESPÉCIES", "SOORTEN") is sized for 8 characters (`COUNT_LABEL_CHARS`), the longest across the languages, with an accented capital's height on the share images (`MONO_CAP_ACCENT`); a new language's label must be 8 characters or fewer. Change the type here and in count-box.ts together. The collage's geometry is CSS px of its container only, never the reader's text size: a bigger text size grows the labels around it and the count's label, capped at 1.25x (15px) and clamped to what the hole holds (`labelBounds`; under page zoom it shrinks with the stage); the number is in cqw and stays put, so text size never repacks the collage.
- **Card name** (Cabinet 700 18px; scientific name in Satoshi 600 italic 16px, `lang` attribute set).
- **Body** (Satoshi 500, 14px in the collage view, 15-16px elsewhere, 1.45-1.5; notes 14px Muted; ledes 16-17px Muted, max 640-720px).
- **Label** `.eb-mono` (Geist Mono 500, 12px, +0.06em, uppercase, Muted, tabular numerals): stats, dates, mass, status lines, footer.
- **Fields** are 16px so iOS does not zoom.
- **Scientific names** are italic and `lang`-tagged; they lead in the card, tooltip, list rows, order and family chips and the breadcrumb when "Scientific" is on.

## Layout

- Page container `.eb-page`: max 1152px, 16px gutters (32px from 720px), vertical gap 40px (56px from 720px); collage page 16/24px.
- Collage block `.eb-view`: one column, 12px between stats line, dock, filters and stage (LifeList's rhythm).
- Breakpoints actually used: 320/359 (top bar squeezes), 480, 560 (compact demo), 600 (filters become one "Choose an order" button + sheet; the controls bar is always two rows: view icons, "True" / "Sci." (or "Heaviest") and the magnifier, then Places, Time and Status), 720 (gutters, hero in a row), 820 (list rows collapse), 880 (three source cards). The controls bar answers to its own width: under 820px the Places and Time buttons take a second row (under 980px when Status makes three), under 600px they split it evenly at 36px (44px hit areas); with three they size by content instead, an active one dropping its glyph (its Accent text and summary say it) so "Places · 2", "Time · 2022–2024" and "Status · 1" all read whole on a 360px phone and wider (on a 320px phone with all three active only Time ellipses: Places and Status never shrink), and under 320px of bar every glyph goes (the labels say it), so the landing's birds keep most of a phone's first screen; its inset is 4px all round (above the tabs, either side of the Hairline, under the buttons). Row one (tabs, tools, magnifier) never breaks: icon tabs under 460px of bar, short labels ("True", "Sci.", "Heaviest") under 540px, tighter under 330px and 310px (36px icon buttons with 44px presses).
- The dock responds to its own width with container queries (460, 540, 330 and 310px; for the drops 1080 or 820, 600 and 320): tab labels hide, "Scientific" shortens, the switch tightens.
- Touch targets 44px (chips are 32px with a 44px `::after` hit area; nav links are drawn 40px, 36-40px on phones, and text buttons 36px, each with a 44px `::after` hit area; the theme switch keeps a 44px hit area when drawn at 40 or 36px; the Places, Time and Status panels' Clear is a text button with the same 44px press; every `.eb-textbtn`'s `::after` hit area is at least 44px wide as well as high). `e2e/flows.spec.ts` checks every page at its sampled widths in all three engines.
- Stage: positions in % of stage width (`cqw`); the stage height eases to the group's height.
- Stage growth: a list too big for the starting stage (wide 1120×700, medium 700×800, narrow 350×900 layout units) grows it taller, never wider, until the median sticker is 48px (wide), 40px (medium) or 30px (narrow) at the layout's width, up to 2:3 (narrow: twice its start). The spiral follows the grown stage's aspect, so a mid-size list (the 548-species sample on desktop) is a round-to-tall cluster around the count, not a wide oval; lists whose median sticker already meets the new floor on the starting stage keep their layouts (the 20/100/200-bird checks); lists that met only the old floors (wide about 200–380 species, medium 200–300, narrow 200–250) now grow a little taller.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth is hairline borders and the Surface tint. Floating things only get one shadow: `0 12px 32px rgb(0 0 0 / .08), 0 2px 6px rgb(0 0 0 / .04)` (bird card, tooltip, search results, toast, the Places, Time and Status popovers, and the share dialog over its .28 scrim); the phone sheet uses `0 -8px 32px rgb(0 0 0 / .12)` over a .28 scrim. Everything else is an inset ring (the switch track). The share dialog had its own heavier `0 16px 48px / .18` until 2026-11-06, when Yusuf brought it in line. `tests/tokens.test.ts` fails on any other shadow in the app's CSS (About's demo scene excepted: it draws its own Stageshow depth).

**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** No shadow on anything that does not float over the page.

**Layers** (the page's z-index ladder, tokens in `tokens.css`). In-page layers stay under 40 and inside their own blocks: the stage's birds (2-4), the controls bar (3) with its search results (8) and the Places, Time and Status popovers (9), the tooltip (30), Tick by hand's sticky bar (2). Over the page, in order: the top bar `--z-topbar` 40; the share dialog's and bird card's scrims `--z-dialog-scrim` 49; the bird card (popover or sheet) `--z-dialog` 50; the undo toast `--z-toast` 60; the phones' bottom sheets (Places, Time, Status, PickerSheet) `--z-sheet-scrim` 950 / `--z-sheet` 951; the first-visit welcome `--z-welcome` 955. A new layer takes a token and a place on this ladder, not a new number. The top bar is under every dialog, scrim, sheet and the toast; it never covers a popover because the popover's controls bar is kept clear of it on open.

## Shapes

**The Squircle Rule.** Corners are superellipses: `border-radius: calc(R * var(--one-if-corner-shape-supported, .566)); corner-shape: superellipse(2)`. The variable is 1 where `corner-shape` is supported and .566 (LifeList's fallback factor) where not. Radii in use: chip/field 8, nav link/button 12, result row 10, theme toggle 14, card/dock-adjacent panels/popovers 16, source card/sheet/tick panel 20. Circles (switch knob) and the 56px list thumbs are exempt, and the Red List pill takes the switch track's full rounding (9px on its 18px height), as a track does.

Silhouette hit-testing (`collage-hit.ts`) makes a sticker clickable only where it is opaque.

### Chips and filters
`.eb-chip`: 32px, Surface, 1px Hairline, 8px squircle, 14px 500, muted count with tabular numerals, **no icon**. Selected: border Accent. Once a level has a choice, its other chips drop to 70% opacity; pressing the selected chip clears it. Mono rank label ("Order", "Family") leads each row; the breadcrumb ("All birds / Perching birds / Crows") has the current place in Accent 600. Taxonomy (orders, then families) is the only filter outside the controls bar; Places, Time and Status are the bar's dropdowns (below). The taxonomy is the same on every page (the landing's sample included): from 600px the rank label and chips wrapping onto as many rows as they need, as /lifelist's; nothing scrolls sideways. The Time dropdown holds the timeline: a strip of months (at least 6px each; a run of 12 or more empty months, such as the years between a lone early lifer and regular birding, collapses to two months' width marked by a slanted break on the baseline; the strip scrolls sideways only below that, with no scrollbar: an edge with more beyond it fades; it opens on the latest months), a bar per month of new species (Muted inside the chosen range, Hairline outside, empty months a baseline tick; a square-root scale over the busiest month with a 12% floor, so one species is a clear bar beside a big trip's month), the range as a Surface band between two handles (a 16px Paper knob ringed in Accent, and once a range is chosen an Accent line through the bars; 44px hit areas that never overflow the strip, sliders for the keyboard), and mono years under it (where they fit). Nothing else: no trip brackets or country labels (removed 2026-11-06, Yusuf: on a big list they filled the panel with a cascade of flags; a trip is a Places country plus a Time range). A mono readout leads ("Mar 2020 to Jun 2021 · 178 species"), wrapping between its parts, never inside a date; once a range is chosen, Clear sits at the head's right, as the Places panel's (the same text button, 36px drawn, a 44px press), and resets the range to the whole span. Under 600px a level is one button ("Choose an order 18", 44px, 12px) opening a bottom sheet of wrapped chips. Hover (fine pointers) raises the border to Line.

## Motion

Source of truth: `src/components/collage/collage-motion.ts` (ported from LifeList).

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| TRAVEL | `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.1,1)`, 640ms | A kept bird moving to its new place; stage height easing |
| EASE_OUT (arrive) | `cubic-bezier(.25,.8,.25,1)`, 480ms starting at 160ms | An arriving bird grows from `translate(12%,12%) scale(.76)` and fades in; card and hover lift |
| EASE_IN | `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)`, 180ms | A leaving bird shrinks and fades |
| Ripple | 0 to 120ms by distance from the count | Delay of each bird's move; first appearance ripples over 0-480ms |
| UI ease | `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` 150-320ms | Chips, switches, sheets, breadcrumb rise (`translateY(-4px)`) |
| Tab opacity | `cubic-bezier(.25,.1,.25,1)` 250ms | Dock/nav/tab resting to pressed |

- **Compositor only.** Birds move by writing CSS variables (`--x --y --k --md`) and animating `translate`/`scale`/`opacity`; unchanged values are skipped; big changes are written in chunks over several frames (cost units: WRITE_START 100, WRITE_MIN 50, WRITE_MAX 800); birds out of the viewport skip their animation.
- **The 1,500 rule.** Above 1,500 visible birds (`CROSSFADE_AT`) a change is a 200ms opacity fade instead of travel (`data-fast`, `data-many`).
- **Reduced motion.** `prefers-reduced-motion` gets the same 200ms fade, no travel, no growth, no stage height easing, no switch transitions; UI transitions go to `none`; sheets and breadcrumbs fade only. About's demo shows its poster.
- Hover/press affordances only under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Optimize pass (impeccable, Task Z, 2026-11-06): nothing to change.** Measured on the production build at 4× CPU throttling: filtering frames at most 33.4 ms, a held timeline drag over 1,000 species 16.8 ms, the True size switch 8.7 ms during a 200,000-row parse; the share preview shows its spinner at once. The `perf` project (`e2e/filters.spec.ts`) holds these to its budget: no task over 50 ms, no frame over 50 ms. The one known gap is the frame budget past 3,000 species, which is its own follow-up.
- The About demo pauses off screen and in a hidden tab, and follows the site theme.
